About the company

Clear thinking for technology decisions

XD ORIGAMI LTD is an information technology consultancy, incorporated in England and Wales on 23 November 2016 under company number 10493206. Its work is IT consultancy: helping organisations understand their technology options, set sensible priorities and move projects forward with clarity.

The company is independent and owner-managed, with no parent group. That keeps things simple: you speak directly with the consultant giving the advice, and recommendations are sized to your organisation rather than to a large firm's overheads.

The name says something about the approach. Origami turns a plain sheet into something useful through a series of precise, well-placed folds. Good IT consultancy works the same way: careful steps, clean structure and nothing unnecessary.

Services

What XD ORIGAMI can help with

Four connected areas of IT consultancy. Each piece of work is scoped to the organisation and the question in front of it.

IT consultancy and strategy

Helping organisations plan and prioritise technology decisions, so time and budget go where they matter most.

  • Review of the current IT setup and the problems it causes
  • A prioritised plan linked to what the organisation needs to achieve
  • An independent view on technology proposals and options

Systems and solution planning

Reviewing requirements and shaping a suitable software or system approach before money is committed.

  • Gathering requirements with the people who use the systems
  • Comparing options on fit, effort and cost
  • Clear written recommendations and next steps

Project support

Hands-on consulting through technology projects, from first scoping to delivery and handover.

  • Scoping, planning and setting realistic milestones
  • Working alongside internal teams and suppliers
  • Regular checkpoints through delivery and handover

Infrastructure and cloud advice

Guidance on IT setup, moving to cloud services and keeping everyday systems dependable.

  • Advice on devices, networks and cloud set-up
  • Planning a sensible move to cloud services
  • Practical steps for day-to-day reliability
